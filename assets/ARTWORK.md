# Sculpture artwork provenance

All six PNGs were created with the built-in image generation tool for this site. They are raster cutouts with alpha transparency, not 3D model files. No stock asset or paid service is required by the website. Their gently moving presentation is implemented with browser transforms and timed crossfades.
